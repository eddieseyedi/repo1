📢 2026 Annual Meeting

The Board of Directors invites all homeowners to attend the 2026 Situs Place Homeowners Association Annual Meeting. This meeting is an opportunity to learn about the Association, hear updates from the Board, review the financial status of the community, and participate in discussions about the future of Situs Place.

Meeting Details

  • Date: Saturday, November 7, 2026
  • Time: 10:30 AM
  • Location: Shawnee Civic Center
  • Breakfast: Continental breakfast will be served beginning at 10:30 AM.

We encourage every homeowner to attend. Your participation and input help make Situs Place a stronger community.
