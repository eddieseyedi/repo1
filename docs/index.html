EXCITING NEWS!

The board voted for Top Rail Fence Company to start fence replacement this spring. Group “E” will have their fences replaced ﬁrst. To ﬁnd out which group you are in, please check the directory map available in our community directory or view the group details here. Homeowners in Group “E” will receive a letter in the mail specifying when replacement will begin. We have also allocated funds to replace two walls this year, with work scheduled to begin in summer. Further details about the wall replacement projects will be shared as the start date approaches. We will need conﬁrmation of your desire to have a gate installed as soon as possible – no later than end of February. The cost of gates: Single Gate - $266 Double Gate - $516 Residents are required to use Top Rail for all gate installations.
