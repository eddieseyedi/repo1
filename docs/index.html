🌞 Situs Place HOA Summer Picnic – June 27

Join your neighbors for a fun and relaxing afternoon at our upcoming Situs Place HOA Community Picnic on Saturday, June 27!

  • 🍔 Burgers and hot dogs will be provided.
  • 🍦 Häagen-Dazs ice cream will also be provided.

Neighborhood Wildlife Alert

We wanted to let everyone know that a coyote was recently spotted in our neighborhood. Please be extra careful with small dogs and pets, especially during early morning and evening hours.

  • Keep pets on a leash when outside.
  • Do not leave small pets unattended outdoors.
  • Stay alert while walking in the neighborhood.

Thank you for helping keep our community and pets safe.

Exciting Community News!

The board has approved Top Rail Fence Company to begin fence replacements this spring. Group “E” will have their fences replaced first. To find out which group your home is in, please check the directory map in the community directory or view the group details here.

Homeowners in Group “E” will receive a letter in the mail with their scheduled replacement date.

We have also allocated funds to replace two walls this year, with work planned to begin this summer. Further details will be shared as the start date approaches.
